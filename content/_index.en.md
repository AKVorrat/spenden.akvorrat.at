title: Donate

As an independent, non-profit civil liberties organisation, epicenter.works relies on outside support. Individual donations from civil society make our work possible: defending fundamental rights and freedoms in the digital age. That's why we're asking for your support.

To give you a better picture of who we are and what we do, we publish an annual [transparency report](https://epicenter.works/en/transparency).

**Regular donations** in particular help us plan our work. We warmly invite you to [become a supporting member](#become-supporter)!

You can find more information on donating in our [donation FAQ](https://epicenter.works/en/transparency) and in our [privacy policy for donations and supporting memberships](https://epicenter.works/en/privacy).
