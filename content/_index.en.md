title: Donate
---

As an independent, non-profit civil rights organization, epicenter.works depends on outside support. Individual donations from civil society make our work defending fundamental rights and freedoms in the digital age possible. That's why we're asking for your support.

To give you a better picture of our organization and its work, we publish an annual [transparency report](https://epicenter.works/en/transparency).

**Recurring donations** in particular help us plan our work. We warmly invite you to [become a supporting member](#become-supporter)!

You can find more information about donating in our [donation FAQ](https://epicenter.works/en/transparency) and in our [privacy policy for donations and supporting memberships](https://epicenter.works/en/privacy).
